# Casio Calculator Issues

An independent community tracker for reproducible bugs in Casio graphing calculators and their official emulators.

[Browse issues](https://github.com/yzhang37/casio-calculator-issues/issues) · [Find your regional CASIO support channel](https://world.casio.com/support/)

## Report a bug

Look for the behavior in [Issues](https://github.com/yzhang37/casio-calculator-issues/issues) first. If there is a match, add your result there. For a different behavior, choose **New issue** and include:

- Exact calculator model and full OS version.
- Physical hardware or emulator. For an emulator, include its name and version.
- The key sequence or smallest sample that reproduces the behavior.
- What you expected and what happened.

Keep workarounds, results from additional models, and public replies from Casio in the issue for that bug.
